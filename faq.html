Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NestX and how to get involved.

What is NestX and what is its purpose? expand_more

NestX is the global innovation network that connects Adventist universities to collaborate, share knowledge, incubate ideas, and create meaningful impact across borders. Our mission is to accelerate innovation with purpose by building bridges between students, faculty, researchers, and entrepreneurs within the Adventist higher-education ecosystem.

Who can join NestX? expand_more

NestX is open to anyone connected to the Adventist university ecosystem. This includes students, faculty members, researchers, administrators, and entrepreneurs affiliated with any of the participating Adventist institutions. We also welcome collaboration from external organizations, mentors, and industry partners who share our vision of purpose-driven innovation.

How can I participate in NestX events and workshops? expand_more

All upcoming events are listed on our Events section. You can register for any open event directly through the website. Events include hands-on workshops, hackathons, webinars, and innovation sprints — most of which are offered online so you can participate from anywhere in the world. You will receive a confirmation email with access details upon registration.

Is there a cost to join or participate in NestX? expand_more

NestX membership and most events are completely free. Our goal is to lower barriers and make innovation accessible to every Adventist university student and professional. Some specialized programs or certifications may have a nominal fee, which will always be clearly communicated ahead of time.

How can my university become a NestX partner? expand_more

We are always looking to expand our network! If your Adventist university or organization is interested in joining, please reach out through the Join the Network form. A member of our team will contact you to discuss partnership opportunities, resource sharing, and how your institution can contribute to and benefit from the NestX ecosystem.
