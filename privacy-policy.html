Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

NestX is the global innovation network connecting Adventist universities to collaborate, share knowledge, incubate ideas, and create meaningful impact across borders. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the rights you have over your data.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide through our forms:

  • Join form: name, email address, institution or organization, country, and message.
  • Event registration: full name, institutional email, university, and role.
  • Newsletter subscription: email address.

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics beacons, or third-party advertising scripts. Any information your browser shares automatically (such as pages visited during your session) is not stored or profiled by us.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide exclusively to:

  • Respond to your messages and contact requests.
  • Manage event registrations and send access links and certificates.
  • Send newsletter updates, opportunities, and community stories (only if you subscribe).
  • Coordinate collaboration between network members and partner universities.

We will never use your information for purposes incompatible with those described here.

4. Legal Basis

We process your personal information based on your consent, which you give when you submit any of our forms, and on our legitimate interest in operating an academic collaboration network. You may withdraw your consent at any time, as described in section 7.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data may only be shared with:

  • The partner university organizing an event you registered for, strictly for event logistics.
  • Authorities, only when required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy: contact messages are retained for up to 24 months, event records for up to 36 months, and newsletter subscriptions until you unsubscribe. Afterwards, data is securely deleted.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw your consent, including unsubscribing from the newsletter at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the Contact option in the footer and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, so we encourage you not to share sensitive personal information through our forms.

9. Children's Privacy

NestX is an academic and professional network intended for university students, faculty, and partners. Our forms are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. External Links

This website may link to partner universities and other external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their own policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our activities or legal requirements. The current version will always be published on this page together with its last-updated date.
